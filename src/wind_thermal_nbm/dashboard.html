Thermal health of the Kelmarsh turbines

Normal behaviour models of nine drivetrain and electrical temperatures on six Senvion MM92 turbines (open Kelmarsh SCADA data, 2019-2021). Each model is trained two years before and calibrated one year before the year shown. Values are daily residuals (measured minus expected) in units of the calibration year's spread: red runs hotter than normal, blue cooler.

Fitted RC parameters (training year 2019)

tau: thermal time constant. Rise: temperature rise above the cooling side at rated power and speed, i.e. thermal resistance x losses - it grows when cooling degrades. These are the detection fits, with free cooling-side weights; the yearly trend in the README constrains them (non-negative, summing to 1).

Data: Kelmarsh wind farm data, C. Plumley and R. Takeuchi (Cubico Sustainable Investments), doi:10.5281/zenodo.16807551, CC BY 4.0. Code: wind-thermal-nbm.
